# Ghana Energy Transition Investment Brief

## Solar Power, Industrial Energy Efficiency and Green Finance Opportunities

> Africa’s energy transition is not only an environmental issue. It is an investment, industrial productivity and development opportunity.

## Project Overview

This project examines investment opportunities arising from Ghana’s energy transition, with particular attention to solar power, industrial energy efficiency and green finance.

The project will combine energy-market data, investment analysis, policy research and financial assessment to identify commercially viable opportunities that can support Ghana’s economic development and transition towards a more sustainable energy system.

## Core Research Question

Where are the most investable opportunities in Ghana’s solar-power and industrial energy-efficiency markets, and how can green finance help mobilize capital for these opportunities?

## Focus Areas

### 1. Solar Power

The solar-power analysis will examine:

- Commercial and industrial rooftop solar
- Utility-scale solar projects
- Solar-plus-battery-storage systems
- Distributed solar and mini-grids
- Solar systems for public institutions
- Power-purchase agreements and equipment-leasing models

### 2. Industrial Energy Efficiency

The industrial energy-efficiency analysis will examine:

- Energy-efficient motors and machinery
- Industrial cooling and refrigeration
- Efficient boilers and process-heating systems
- Energy monitoring and smart-metering systems
- Building-energy management
- Energy-service company and shared-savings models

### 3. Green Finance

The green-finance analysis will examine:

- Green bonds
- Sustainability-linked loans
- Blended finance
- Concessional finance
- Credit guarantees
- Renewable-energy investment funds
- Carbon-finance opportunities
- The potential role of Ghana’s capital market

## Project Objectives

The project aims to:

1. Assess Ghana’s current energy-transition landscape.
2. Identify commercially attractive solar-power opportunities.
3. Examine energy-efficiency opportunities for Ghanaian industries.
4. Evaluate suitable green-finance instruments.
5. Review relevant investment laws, policies and regulations.
6. Identify major financial, regulatory and implementation risks.
7. Develop practical investment concepts for investors and financial institutions.

## Planned Outputs

The completed project will include:

## Proposed Investment Concepts

The project will develop three initial investment concepts:

1. **Commercial and Industrial Solar Facility**  
   A financing platform for rooftop solar and battery systems for Ghanaian businesses.

2. **Industrial Energy-Efficiency Facility**  
   A performance-based financing programme for industrial energy-saving technologies.

3. **Ghana Green Investment Facility**  
   A blended-finance vehicle combining private investment, local bank financing, development finance and guarantees.

## Methodology

The analysis will use:

- Publicly available energy and economic data
- Government policies and regulatory documents
- Industry and institutional reports
- Financial and investment indicators
- Comparative market analysis
- Risk and opportunity assessment

Historical data will generally cover 2015 to the latest verified year available. Policy and regulatory information will be updated to the project’s publication date.
