# Project Source Tracker

## Purpose

This document records the sources used in the Ghana Energy Transition Investment Brief. Every important statistic, policy statement and investment claim should be traceable to a reliable source.

## Source Selection Rules

1. Prioritize official government and regulatory sources.
2. Use the latest available data.
3. Record the publication date and the period covered.
4. Distinguish reported information from personal calculations.
5. Avoid using unsupported statistics.
6. Record any limitations affecting the reliability of a source.

## Source Categories

- Ghana’s electricity system
- Solar-power market
- Industrial energy consumption
- Electricity tariffs
- Energy policies and regulations
- Investment laws and incentives
- Green finance and capital markets
- Carbon markets and climate finance
- Macroeconomic and financial indicators
- Development-finance programmes

## Verification Status

Use one of the following labels:

- **Verified** — information confirmed directly from a reliable source
- **Partially verified** — some information still requires confirmation
- **To review** — source has not yet been fully examined
- **Rejected** — source is unreliable, outdated or unsuitable

## Notes on Calculations

Any calculations produced for this project should record:

- Original data source
- Formula used
- Units of measurement
- Currency and exchange rate
- Base year, where applicable
- Assumptions
- Limitations
